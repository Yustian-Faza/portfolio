01 / 06 Operational Dashboard — Fleet Logistics

Vehicle Service Monitoring Dashboard

An Excel monitoring system that flags which fleet vehicles are on schedule, overdue, or not yet serviced.

  • 334Vehicles tracked
  • 8 linked sheetsPipeline
  • Built soloStatus, in use

MethodExcel · Data validation

Service records were collected manually from branches across the country. The workflow reshapes monthly submissions from wide to long format, checks them against the master fleet list, flags potential data issues, and feeds a dashboard showing which vehicles are on schedule, overdue, or not yet serviced. The monitoring rule is 180 days or 10,000 kilometers since the last service. The data shown is synthetic and does not contain FIFGROUP’s actual records.

Internship project, FIFGROUP. All names, branch codes, and identifiers in the workbook are synthetic.

02 / 06 SQL Analytics — Retail

Chinook Revenue Analysis

Tracing where a digital music store’s revenue actually comes from, entirely in SQL.

  • 2.13%Top customer of revenue
  • 73.5%Top 4 genres of revenue
  • 100% passData-quality checks

MethodSQL

The analysis begins with data-quality checks such as comparing invoice totals with their line items and looking for orphaned records. Revenue turned out not to be heavily concentrated among a few customers: the largest accounted for 2.13% of the total, while 80% of revenue came from 47 of 59 customers. Genre had a stronger effect — Rock, Latin, Metal, and Alternative & Punk made up more than 73% of sales.

03 / 06 Time-Series Forecasting — Capital Markets

Hybrid ICEEMDAN–SVD–LSTM Stock Forecasting

A decomposition-and-denoising pipeline that forecasts the closing prices of three Indonesian stocks from different sectors.

  • 16.46–70.85RMSE range
  • up to 85.9%MAPE ↓ vs. LSTM
  • 2026Published

MethodICEEMDAN · SVD · LSTM

Stock prices are noisy and change over time, which makes them hard to forecast directly. The pipeline decomposes each series with ICEEMDAN, denoises the components with SVD, and models each component with its own LSTM. Across three stocks from different sectors it was compared with seven other model combinations and achieved the lowest error for all three, while a Diebold–Mariano test indicated the improvement was statistically significant. Published in Jurnal Statistika (Vol. 19, 2026) as part of my undergraduate thesis in Statistics at Universitas Gadjah Mada.

With Vemmie Nastiti Lestari.

04 / 06 Classification, Deployed — Healthcare

Symptom-Based Disease Prediction

A symptom checker that asks fewer questions by choosing each next question with information gain.

  • CatBoostMulticlass model
  • Information gainQuestion logic
  • Live appStatus

MethodCatBoost · Information gain · Streamlit

Instead of a fixed list of questions, the app chooses the next question based on information gain. A CatBoost multiclass classifier, evaluated with stratified cross-validation, updates the prediction as more symptoms are provided, and the final screen shows the predicted disease with a short description and precautions. I built and deployed the full pipeline for a Statistical Machine Learning course project.

Not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis — a course project on applied classification.

05 / 06 Time-Series Forecasting — Food & Agriculture Policy

Forecasting Indonesia’s Milk Production & Cattle Population

Projecting cattle population and milk output to 2029 against the government’s free-milk program for schoolchildren.

  • CNN, MSE 0.0039Best model
  • 52 years, FAOTraining data
  • ≈7.7M tons2029 gap

MethodCNN · LSTM · Bidirectional LSTM

I forecast cattle population and milk production through 2029 using CNN, LSTM, and Bidirectional LSTM models trained on 52 years of FAO data, evaluated with 5-fold cross-validation. A 1D CNN gave the lowest error for both series. The resulting forecast suggests a sizeable gap between projected production and demand by 2029, estimated at around 7.7 million tons. This was a group final project for a Forecasting Methods course at Universitas Gadjah Mada.

Group project with Cintya Kusumawardhani and Fabima Fadhlin Minallah Zidta.

06 / 06 Unsupervised Clustering — Education Policy

Clustering Districts by Education Access — Kabupaten Demak

Grouping the 14 districts of Kabupaten Demak by how accessible their schools are.

  • 0.57 vs. 0.38Silhouette (K-Means vs. K-Medoids)
  • 4Clusters
  • 14Districts

MethodPCA · K-Means · K-Medoids

Using school counts and teacher–student ratios across four education levels, I applied PCA before comparing K-Means and K-Medoids. K-Means gave the better result, with a Silhouette Score of 0.57 against 0.38. The four clusters show that the education gap is not uniform: some areas have fewer schools, while others face more of a teacher-availability issue.

Statistics internship (Kerja Praktik), BPS Kabupaten Demak.
